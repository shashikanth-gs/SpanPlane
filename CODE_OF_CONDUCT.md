# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want SpanPlane to be a welcoming, respectful collaboration space for people with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and technical perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific when discussing code, protocol behavior, and design decisions.
- Assume good intent while making room for correction.
- Respect privacy: do not share credentials, private endpoint details, or sensitive agent output.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, never people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository’s private GitHub Security Advisory flow or directly to the project maintainers through a private channel. Reports will be handled discreetly and without retaliation.
